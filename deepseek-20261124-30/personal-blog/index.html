02 / A little context

Designing for the way
a place feels.

I’m an independent designer working between brand, editorial, and the built environment. My favorite projects start with a walk and a sketchbook.

After a decade in studios in Shanghai and New York, I’ve learned that the smallest details do the heaviest lifting: a well-placed bench, the wording on a menu, the pause before a page turns. This site is where I keep track of those details.
