How the sun model works

A clear-sky NOAA approximation uses the selected city’s latitude, longitude, and local clock time. Terrain, buildings, clouds, and reflected light are not modelled. Times include the location’s daylight-saving offset.

  • Shadow edges are softened; sun/shade labels use ray tests against the wall, roof, railing, and furniture.
  • Planters are assessed at three heights to distinguish full sun, dappled light, and shade.
