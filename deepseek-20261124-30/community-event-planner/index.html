    Documentation

    Simplified walking model

    Grid. A 1 m × 1 m occupancy grid covers the 68 m × 44 m park. A cell is blocked when it falls within an equipment footprint plus 0.45 m pedestrian clearance, a tree trunk clearance, the hedge, or a park boundary.

    Search. Routes use four-direction A* from the chosen gate to a walkable cell at the selected facility's edge. Distances are measured along the resulting grid route in metres and recomputed whenever the layout or route setting changes.

    Access modes. Pedestrian mode permits the fixed stepped shortcut through the central hedge. Step-free mode removes those step cells and relies on the level-access gap near the east boundary. The stepped shortcut is never available in step-free mode.

    Limits. This is a demonstrative, simplified route model. It does not model gradients, surfacing, door widths, crowding, emergency egress, or regulatory requirements, and it does not establish accessibility compliance.
