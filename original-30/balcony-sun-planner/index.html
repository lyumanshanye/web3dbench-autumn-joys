How the light is calculated

A useful estimate,
not a light meter.

Sunroom runs a simplified solar-position model entirely in your browser. No location permission or API key is needed.

01

Solar position

Day-of-year declination and the equation of time give altitude and azimuth. Sample cities use a fixed standard UTC offset, so daylight saving is not applied.

02

Cast shadows

The wall, roof slab, rail, furniture, and plants are real 3D geometry. A directional light with shadow mapping produces the visible shadow pattern.

03

Planter status

For each planter, a ray is traced from its canopy toward the sun. If balcony geometry blocks the ray, the planter is marked as shaded.

04

Known limits

The model ignores atmospheric refraction, weather, nearby buildings, trees, and reflections. Treat it as a directional guide, then verify in place.
