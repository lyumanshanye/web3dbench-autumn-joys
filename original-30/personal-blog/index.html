A little context

Design is how I pay attention.

I’m a product designer by day and a committed noticer the rest of the time. I live in Brooklyn with too many cookbooks and a south-facing window that makes one square of floor excellent.

This journal is where I follow a question until it turns into a walk, a recipe, or a small change to the room. No growth hacks. Just useful observations, recorded while they’re still warm.
