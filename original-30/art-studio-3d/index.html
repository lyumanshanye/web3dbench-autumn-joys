A place for curious objects

We make the unreal feel touchable.

Norb is an independent 3D art studio operating somewhere between sculpture, motion and code. We build visual worlds with weight, texture and a mind of their own.

Small by design. Fast by instinct. Always chasing the image we have not seen before.

Study 014 / Synthetic tactility

Our point of view / 01

Perfect is a closed door. We are more interested in what happens when form gets a little restless.

Every Norb project begins with a question: what should this thing make people feel? Then we build outward — material by material, frame by frame — until the answer becomes something you can almost hold.
