A LITTLE CONTEXT

Follow the sun, thoughtfully.

This is a simplified, clear-sky sun study. Sample city coordinates, solar declination and hour angle determine the light direction. Times are local solar time, not clock time.

The balcony's walls, overhang, furniture and railing cast shadows. Planter labels use a ray from the center of their foliage toward the sun. The study doesn't account for clouds, neighboring buildings or seasonal plant growth.
