FIELD NOTES / SELECTED WORK

I work slowly. Walking before lifting the camera, returning to the same place at different hours, waiting for a detail to reveal itself. A shift of light. The shape of silence. The moment an ordinary landscape becomes unfamiliar.

“Sometimes the most important part of an image is what you leave outside the frame.”

These images are an invitation to pause. To spend a little longer looking. And, perhaps, to carry that attention back into the everyday.
