1. Replace the debug page's random house and single-tile scene contents with one `THREE.InstancedMesh` named `debug-tiles`, using `CylinderGeometry(1, 1, 1, 6)` or the equivalent buffer geometry and flat Lambert material. Preserve the existing lights, renderer, resize behavior, and OrbitControls polar limits. Clicking a tile selects it; clicking empty space clears selection; drags over 5 pixels and non-left buttons change nothing. 3.
